Project Overview:
My project will simulate a the growth of a garden over the course of a 90 day season.
The software will track the growth of various plants based on soil conditions which change
due to weather events.

Implementation:
A map will be used to categorize different plant species. Each plant species will be 
associated with an array that stores three lists of positions, plant growth, and soil conditions.
Positions will be stored as a struct holding an x and y coordinate, plant growth will be stored
as a percentage of maximum growth, and soil quality will be stored as a struct holding a
water level and nutrient level.
Each plant species will be a Plant class object, with differing ideal water levels and nutrient levels.

A 2D array of positions and associated plants will also be stored to visually represent plants

Simulated Events:
The simulation will track the growth of plants each day based on nutrient and water levels.
Weather events will occur that change water or nutrient levels, with different seasons having
differing rates of weather events. The user will have the ability to control water maintenence
via sprinklers at different positions which water plants within a certain radius, as well as 
fertilization periods.
