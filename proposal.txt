Project Overview:
My project will simulate a the growth of a garden over the course of a 90 day season.
The software will track the growth of various plants on a garden plot, whose conditions change
due to weather events.

Implementation:
A map will be used to categorize different plant species. Each plant species will be 
associated with an array that stores growth level, water level, and nutrient level, all stored as percentages.
Each plant species will be associated with a plant species object, with differing ideal water levels and nutrient levels.

Simulated Events:
The simulation will track the growth of plants each day based on nutrient and water levels.
Weather events will occur that change water or nutrient levels, with different seasons having
differing rates of weather events. The user will have the ability to specify water and 
fertilization amounts and frequencies.
